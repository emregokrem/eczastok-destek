# EczaStok Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 25, 2026

This policy explains how the EczaStok mobile application (the "App") handles data. The App is
developed by Emre Gökrem (the "Developer").

**In short:** The App has no accounts, does not collect personal data, and contains no ads or
analytics/tracking. Camera images and your count lists stay on your device.

## 1. Camera

The App uses the camera to read medicine DataMatrix codes and barcodes. Images are processed in
real time on the device by the operating system's barcode recognition components (Apple Vision
on iOS, Google ML Kit on Android). **Images are not stored and are never sent to any server.**

On Android, Google ML Kit may send anonymous performance and usage metrics (e.g. device model,
OS version, processing time) to Google. These metrics do not contain camera images or the
content of scanned codes. See [Google ML Kit terms](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms).

## 2. Data stored on your device

The following data is stored **only on your device**, in the App's private storage, and is not
sent to the Developer or any third party:

- Count lists (scanned DataMatrix codes, barcodes and quantities)
- Lists you import for comparison
- App settings (theme, sound, etc.)
- A copy of the downloaded medicine name/price list
- Your subscription status and expiry date

You can delete this data from within the App (clear list) or entirely by uninstalling the App.

## 3. Medicine price list

To show medicine names and prices, the App checks a publicly available medicine price list
server at launch and downloads the current list when needed. No personal information or count
data is sent in this request; however, as with any internet request, the server can see your
device's IP address and standard connection information.

## 4. Export and import

When you export a list, the file is created on your device and the system share sheet opens.
**You decide** where the file goes (email, messaging, cloud storage, etc.); those services are
governed by their own privacy policies. Imported files are read only on the device.

## 5. Purchases and subscription

Subscription payments are processed by the **Apple App Store** or **Google Play**. Your payment
and card details are never shared with the App or the Developer. The App only learns from the
store whether your subscription is active and when it expires. See
[Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) · [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 6. Data we do not collect

The App does **not** collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, device
identifiers or advertising identifier. There are no ads, third-party analytics or tracking tools.

## 7. Children

The App is a professional tool for pharmacy staff and is not directed to children under 13.

## 8. Your rights

Since the App does not collect personal data, the Developer holds no records about you. For any
questions under applicable data protection laws (including Turkey's KVKK and the GDPR), contact
us at the address below.

## 9. Changes

This policy may be updated. The current version is always published on this page; significant
changes are noted in the App's release notes.
